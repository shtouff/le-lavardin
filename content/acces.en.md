**Le Lavardin**
La Jarne, Charente-Maritime, France

We are located just 7 km from La Rochelle, in the village of La Jarne.

**By car**

From La Rochelle, take the D939 road towards Rochefort. After a few kilometres, turn off towards La Jarne. We will send you the exact address and GPS coordinates when you book.

**By train**

The nearest train station is La Rochelle Ville, well-connected to Paris and other major cities. From there, it's a short taxi ride or car hire to reach us.

**By plane**

La Rochelle – Île de Ré Airport (LRH) is just a few kilometres from La Rochelle, with direct flights to several European cities.

Don't hesitate to [contact us](/en/contact/) if you need further directions — we'll be happy to help you plan your journey!

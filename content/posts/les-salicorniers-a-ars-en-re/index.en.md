Let me share one of my favourite shops: Les Salicorniers in Ars-en-Ré. Eddy always welcomes you with a smile and a warm word. The products are wonderful — especially the *sel fou* (seasoned salt)! — and the quality is excellent. You'll find flavoured and plain salts, salted caramel, pickled samphire, and so much more… Eddy also has an online shop, but I really do recommend visiting in person if you're in the area: [http://www.les-salicorniers.fr/](http://www.les-salicorniers.fr/)

You won't be disappointed.

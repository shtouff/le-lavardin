Breakfast is often what you remember long after a stay. Here, we put our heart into it.

Every morning you'll find fresh pastries (croissants, pain au chocolat), bread, butter and homemade jam, yoghurts, fruit juice, and of course coffee, tea or hot chocolate — whatever takes your fancy.

All served on the veranda or out in the garden whenever the weather allows, because there's no better start to a holiday than a coffee in the sunshine under the palm trees!

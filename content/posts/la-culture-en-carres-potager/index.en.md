For our square foot gardening project, I've been using the *Guide pratique du potager en carrés* by Anne-Marie Nageleisen, published by Ulmer. It's a beautifully put-together book, very visual and practical. It focuses especially on planning tools for your crops, with individual growing guides for 30 vegetables and 5 aromatic herbs.

Highly recommended!

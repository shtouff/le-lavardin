The only point of personal data collection on this site is the **contact form**. The data entered is:

## Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

- **Right of access**: obtain a copy of the data held about you
- **Right of rectification**: correct inaccurate data
- **Right to erasure**: request deletion of your data
- **Right to object**: object to the processing of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at: **contact.lelavardin@gmail.com**

In the event of a dispute, you may lodge a complaint with the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr) (French data protection authority).

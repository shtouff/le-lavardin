**Energy management**

Our accommodations are fitted with energy-efficient light bulbs and appliances.

**Resource and waste management**

Compost bins available; guests sort their waste at source using colour-coded recycling bins.

Reduced packaging wherever possible.

Low-flow water restrictors fitted throughout.

Cleaning products used are eco-certified.

**Environmental policy**

We use environmentally friendly products for all cleaning and maintenance.

We favour natural, organic produce and local artisan suppliers for our breakfast ingredients.

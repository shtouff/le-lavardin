Located in the Charente-Maritime, just 7 km from La Rochelle, we warmly welcome you to our home, "Le Lavardin"!

We're Caroline and Rémi, and we're delighted to host you in this beautiful Charentaise house in La Jarne. We fell completely in love with it when we bought it in 2018. Its lovely landscaped garden, along with the covered heated pool (available in season), will make your stay a real pleasure.

Depending on what suits you best, we offer: [a bed & breakfast room called "La Touline"](/en/rooms/la-touline/) and [a holiday cottage called "Le Cabestan"](/en/rooms/le-cabestan/).

Passionate sailors and motorcycle enthusiasts ourselves, we're happy to share our favourite spots — cycling routes, sailing trips, or cultural outings — and point you to the best local addresses.

Committed to protecting the environment, "Le Lavardin" is proud to follow [a green approach](/en/our-commitments/).

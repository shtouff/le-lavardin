Le Cabestan is a charming independent 30 m² holiday cottage, beautifully decorated with wood, with its own private garden. It features a 160 cm double bed, a sofa, a shower room and separate toilet.

The kitchen is fully equipped with an oven, microwave, electric hob, coffee maker, kettle and all the crockery and utensils you'll need for your stay.

Outside, a garden lounge set and barbecue are waiting for you. In season, you'll have access to our covered heated pool (12 m × 6 m).

An extra double sofa bed can be added on the mezzanine, and a travel cot is also available on request. Bed linen (sheets, pillowcases, duvet), a toiletry kit (towels, shampoo, shower gel) and a kitchen starter pack (sponge, tea towels, washing-up liquid) are all provided.

We can prepare a welcome tray of bread, charcuterie and cheese for your arrival day, as an optional extra.

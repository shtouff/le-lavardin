La Touline is a spacious 25 m² bed & breakfast room. It comes with its own shower room and toilet. The cosy little lounge area lets you relax in front of the TV or enjoy some quiet reading. The kitchenette is equipped with a fridge, electric hob, microwave, and a welcome tray (kettle, coffee maker, tea and coffee, sugar).

Outside, you have complete independence with a private entrance. Deck chairs are available if you'd like to unwind in the landscaped garden, and on warm evenings a barbecue awaits you on the wooden terrace. In season, you'll have access to our covered heated pool (12 m × 6 m).

On arrival, just let us know your breakfast preferences. We can either share it together or serve it in your room — whichever you prefer. On fine days, it can be enjoyed in the veranda adjoining the room, overlooking the fish pond.

A travel cot is available on request. Bed linen and a toiletry kit (sheets, bath towels, shampoo, shower gel) are provided.

We can also prepare a welcome tray of organic bread, charcuterie and cheese for your arrival day, as an optional extra.
